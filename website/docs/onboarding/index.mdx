# Onboarding

This guide is for a **new project**: how to build your project/knowledge graph from scratch, how to register
into the **federation**, and how to **share knowledge and collaborate** with other projects once joined.

## Three steps

1. **Create a project** — set up a workspace and an intent graph (ArchiMate/ontology elements, relationships,
   views) and pass local acceptance.
   → [Create a project](./create)
2. **Join the federation** — register as a member at the center, get a globally unique `projectId`, and have the
   center build your knowledge replica.
   → [Join the federation](./join-federation)
3. **Share & collaborate** — authorize others, query other projects' knowledge, contribute subgraphs.
   → [Collaborate](./collaborate)

## Glossary

- **Ontology**: the type system and rules of a graph (e.g., ArchiMate 3.2). See the [ontology shelf](/ontologies).
- **Intent graph**: a project's architecture and knowledge, at `design/KG/SystemArchitecture.json`.
- **Federation / center**: the mechanism that organizes many autonomous projects and lets them share knowledge
  under authorization.
- **projectId**: a member project's globally unique identity in the federation.
